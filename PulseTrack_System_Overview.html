1. Client Requirements Implemented

1. Dynamic Yield Formula Verified

Yield is computed strictly on Physical Material Processed × Lab Yield %. Lab enters byproduct percentages (Split % & Husk %); the main product auto-derives to 100%.

Main Yield % = 100% − Split% − Husk%
Destination Silo Wt = Processed Qty × Main Yield %

Example: 30 Tons @ 10% Split, 3% Husk → 26.1T to Silo 8, 3.0T to Split Silo, 0.9T to Husk Silo.

2 & 3. Dual Moisture Pools (I/P & O/P) Verified

Moisture deductions operate on a 10% Base Threshold with separate pools for Input (I/P) and Output (O/P).

If Moisture ≤ 10%: Deduction = 0 kg (Full weight retained)
If Moisture > 10%: Deduction = Weight × (Moisture% − 10) / 100

Example: 30T @ 13% I/P → 29.1T net (3% moisture deduction). 30T @ 12% O/P → 29.4T deducted from stock.

4. Silo Idle Time Tracking Real-Time

Automatically monitors inactive storage lines:

Idle Duration = Current Timestamp − Last Activity Timestamp

Formatted into human-friendly metrics: Active now, 14m idle, 4h 25m idle, 2d 3h idle. Silos idle for >8 hours trigger red operational warnings.

5. Automated Ledger & Adjustments Audited

Stock balances are maintained by an atomic transaction ledger:

  • Automated: Raw intake credits, milling transfer deductions, and multi-silo yield credits happen instantly.
  • Manual Corrections: Physical count audits and direct silo balancing require operator notes and supervisor audit logging.
